Hello Team,

The season is nearing an end. The progress from the start of the season to now has been amazing. We have one final objective as a team. Each of you has a goal of competing at the highest level you possibly can for one more tournament. Nothing in life is easy or free. This one is going to require everyone on the team to do their part. Embrace whatever role you have and do the best you can. That is all the coaches can ask.

Provided you put in your full effort, whatever happens doesn't matter. You can leave this season knowing that you gave it your all, embraced the team, and the result (whatever that is) is a collective team effort. No one player will define our season. We will define our season as a unit.

The last set of practices this week and weekend are going to be work. Come ready to skate hard and compete. Next week practices will be lighter to allow you to rest leading into the weekend.

See you on the ice!
