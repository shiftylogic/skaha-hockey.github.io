While a bit disappointing, there was some positive to takeaway from the weekend. We do still have some work to do leading into our next tournament and the State Championships.


This week, we will continue to work on ironing out the kinks in our push towards a 2-1-2 forecheck. There will also be some emphasis on creating contact as means to protecting the puck and stopping plays. Be ready to get bumped around a bit this week.

The teams we play this coming weekend have revenge on their minds. Practice hard, rest up, and come ready to play.
