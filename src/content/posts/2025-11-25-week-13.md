We have a single practice on Tuesday this week, along with the 2 extra practices we got this past weekend. Thanks to all the players that came to these optional practices.

This week and next week, the focus is going to be on better angling and defending in layers. We will also re-emphasize team play and moving the puck quickly, using all your available options. We don't have a lot of practices between now and our next tournament at the end of December.

Have a great Thanksgiving and get some rest. Also, hope to see several of you at the Kraken game on Wednesday. Especially for live games, pay attention to the way players are moving around, defending, forechecking, and finding space. It is a great learning tool.

See everyone on the ice!
